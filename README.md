# 🏛️ VANI

> Empowering citizens with AI-driven access to government services through voice, language, and intelligent automation.

VANI is an AI-powered multilingual e-Governance assistant designed to bridge the gap between citizens and government services. It enables users to interact naturally through voice or text, understand government forms, extract information from documents, and receive intelligent assistance in their preferred language.

The platform aims to make public services more accessible, especially for individuals who face language, literacy, or digital accessibility barriers.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Millions of citizens struggle to navigate complex government procedures due to language differences, paperwork, and lack of digital literacy.

VANI simplifies this experience by acting as an intelligent digital assistant that can:

🗣️ Understand voice commands

🌍 Communicate in multiple languages

📄 Read and analyze official documents

🤖 Assist users in completing forms

⚡ Provide quick and accurate responses

---
